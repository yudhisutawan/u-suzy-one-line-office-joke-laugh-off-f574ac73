# Tournament: One-Line Office Joke Laugh-Off

Judged ONLY by the judge's five criteria, in order:
1. Is a one-line office joke (anything else loses to any office joke).
2. Real laugh, sharp surprising punchline.
3. Clear on one read, no setup needing explanation.
4. Fresh, no office cliches everyone has heard.
5. Tight wording, every word earning its place.

All 20 entries are one-line office jokes, so criterion 1 never separated them;
every matchup below was decided on criteria 2–5. Margin: 1 = by a hair,
2 = clearly better.

## Winner

**C8c — "I told my boss I needed a mental health day. He asked if lunch would be long enough."**
84 characters, 84 bytes.

- Undefeated (7–0) in the final round robin and 5–0 in round 4.
- Beat its own original draft (C8) head to head in both rounds.
- **2-win margin over the nearest independent entry** (H3c, 5–2); the only
  entry within 1 win is its own weaker draft C8 (6–1).
- Why it wins on the criteria: (2) the punchline is a sharp, surprising
  absurdity — a whole day squeezed into lunch; (3) instantly clear, no setup
  beyond a familiar phrase; (4) fresh — the "mental health day" ask is new
  territory, not a recycled meeting/email cliche; (5) 84 characters, every
  word earning its place.
